SimPlayer Support

SimPlayer is a local music player and internet radio app for iOS. It plays MP3s (and other common formats) from your own library, plus internet radio stations by stream address, with playlists and favorites.

Common questions

  • A radio station won't play. Double-check the stream address — SimPlayer supports direct MP3/AAC/OGG/FLAC stream URLs, as well as .pls/.m3u/.xspf playlist files. HLS (.m3u8) streams aren't supported.
  • A song won't play or is missing from my library. Use the Rescan button on the Library tab after adding new files to your chosen music folder.
  • I want to delete a playlist or station. Long-press (or swipe) the item in its list for Edit/Delete options.
