Privacy Policy

SimPlayer · Last updated November 21, 2026

SimPlayer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, analytics, or crash reports of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Your music library stays on your device. SimPlayer never uploads your files, filenames, or metadata anywhere.
  • Internet radio stations you add are stored only on your device, and are used only to connect directly to the stream address you provide — no information about your listening is sent to us or any third party.

No tracking

SimPlayer contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. SimPlayer does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.
